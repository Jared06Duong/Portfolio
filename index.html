About Me

I’m a Computer Engineering Co-op student at the University of Guelph focused on embedded systems, FPGA design, and low-level software.

In manufacturing, I worked with live sensor data and production monitoring systems, helping reduce downtime by 15% and improve output consistency by 10%. That experience shaped how I approach engineering.

My projects reflect that focus. I’ve designed and verified a VHDL-based ALU on FPGA, built a Java and MySQL management system with clean data flows, and developed an Arduino-based autonomous wheelchair prototype. I work mainly in C, C++, Java, Python, VHDL, and SQL, and I’m comfortable moving between hardware and software when needed.
