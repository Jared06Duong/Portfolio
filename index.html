About Me

I’m a third-year Computer Engineering co-op student at the University of Guelph. I enjoy building things, understanding how they work, and figuring out why they don’t work the first time.

My work spans software and hardware: a 16-bit ALU in VHDL, a 64-LED cube with a custom PCB, an Arduino-based autonomous wheelchair prototype, and a university management system built with Java and MySQL. These projects have given me hands-on practice with design, debugging, and testing.

At Appleby North Paint, I worked on REST API integration. At Hartmann Canada, I worked around automated production equipment and helped troubleshoot production issues with operators and engineers. I’m also part of the University of Guelph Robotics Team, where I design and prototype mechanical components and help integrate them with electrical and software systems.

I’m looking for Winter 2027 and Summer 2027 co-ops in software development, embedded systems, hardware, firmware, automation, and robotics. Outside engineering, I enjoy soccer, working out, and travelling.
